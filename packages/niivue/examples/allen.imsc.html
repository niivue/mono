Integrated Mitotic Stem Cell

 

About this demo

This page rebuilds, on NiiVue, the two viewers of the Allen Institute for Cell Science essay Integrated Mitotic Stem Cell, which follows human induced pluripotent stem cells through mitosis.

3D Cell Viewer: for each mitotic stage, one atlas holds DNA plus 15 tagged structures, each imaged in a different cell and aligned into one 256x256x58 volume. These atlases are copies in niivue-demo-images-lfs (Allen/IMSC), because imsc.allencell.org does not allow other sites to fetch its files.

Cell Gallery: the same 75 cells as the essay's z-stack viewer (15 genes by 5 stages), each loaded at full resolution from its Cell Feature Explorer atlas in the Allen Institute's own storage, with membrane, structure and DNA as separate channels plus their segmentations.

The data are from the Allen Institute for Cell Science and are used under the Allen Institute's terms of use: noncommercial, with citation. See the LICENSE in the niivue-demo-images-lfs repository.
