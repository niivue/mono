OHIF's measurement tools work on a NiiVue viewport. Pick a tool in the
toolbar and draw on a 2D slice. When the viewport shows a DICOM series, the
finished shape also appears as a row in OHIF's Measurements panel, with its
numbers.

## Which tools work

| Tool | How to draw | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | drag | the length, drawn as a ruler with tick marks |
| Ellipse, Rectangle, Circle | drag | area and intensity |
| Freehand | drag | the outline only |
| Spline, Livewire | click each point | area and intensity |
| Bidirectional | two drags | the long and short diameters |
| Arrow | drag | a label |

Intensity means the minimum, mean, maximum and standard deviation inside
the shape. NiiVue's Ruler button picks the Length tool.

## Drawing shapes

Most tools are a single press, drag and release.

For Spline and Livewire, click to place each point, then double-click to
close the shape. Press Escape to throw away a shape you are still drawing.

For Bidirectional, drag the long axis first, then the short axis across it,
on the same slice. Starting the second drag on another slice starts the
measurement over.

## Units

Lengths are in millimeters and areas in square millimeters, both measured
in the plane of the slice using the real voxel size. Intensity is in the
series' own unit: Hounsfield units for CT, SUV for PET, and no unit
otherwise.

## Using the Measurements panel

Edit a row's label in the panel, and the new label appears next to the
shape in the viewport.

Clicking a row brings its shape back into view in the NiiVue viewport, as
long as nv-ohif is listed after the Cornerstone extension.

Each row points back to its DICOM series. A shape drawn on one of the
slices as they were acquired also points to that exact DICOM image; one
drawn across slices or on a reformatted view does not.

## When a measurement is not in the panel

- **The viewport shows a NIfTI file.** The shape is drawn and measured, but
  rows need a DICOM series behind the viewport.
- **The outline is in pieces or has a hole.** Freehand, Spline and Livewire
  rows need one outline without holes. A shape in pieces stays on the
  viewport, measured, but is not listed.
- **It is an angle.** Angle and Cobb Angle measure on the viewport only.
